# AI Governance Framework

This framework defines governance practices for Agentic AI Operating Systems and autonomous AI workforces.

## Governance Objectives

- Keep humans accountable for sensitive outcomes.
- Make agent behavior reviewable.
- Preserve privacy, confidentiality and business integrity.
- Align automation with business policies.
- Maintain clear ownership for workflows, data and approvals.

## Roles

- Business owner: accountable for workflow outcomes.
- System owner: accountable for implementation and reliability.
- Data owner: accountable for data access and classification.
- Security owner: accountable for controls and risk review.
- Human approver: accountable for sensitive action approval.
- Operator: monitors runs, incidents and evaluation results.

## Policy Areas

- Data classification
- Access control
- Human approval gates
- Allowed and blocked tools
- External communication rules
- Logging and retention
- Evaluation requirements
- Incident response
- Change management

## Governance Checklist

- Is every workflow owned by a named business function?
- Are sensitive actions explicitly listed?
- Are approval gates enforced by the system, not only by prompt instructions?
- Are source systems and data classes documented?
- Are audit logs sufficient for review?
- Are failure modes documented?
- Are evaluation metrics reviewed on a defined cadence?
- Are users trained on what the agent can and cannot do?
